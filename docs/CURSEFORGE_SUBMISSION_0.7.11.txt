RELEASE CONTENT
- Five-second combat checks for recent damage occurrences affecting visible,
  hostile, PvP-flagged players or NPCs still in combat. NPC activity intentionally
  qualifies; the attacker is not identified. Current observer position is used.
- Ordinary unflagged mobs, controlled pets, healing, target selection and damage
  to self alone do not qualify. No native damage-meter reads during combat.
- Bounded events and 32 recipients per window; no idle scan or 100-yard radar.
- Strict post-combat player-damage verification remains the fallback, including
  short fights and death. Both paths share cooldown and one-attempt safeguards.
- Prior session-update history prevents delayed earlier sessions from appearing
  fresh in a later fight. Duplicate binding headers and diagnostics are fixed.
- Live event delivery/field readability still require in-game verification.
