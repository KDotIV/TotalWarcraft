Warband-PvP helps you quickly form parties to seek out world PvP or rally your faction to retaliate against enemy attacks in WoW Forever.

Report the attack. Rally a response. Move together.

Open /wb for your command board. Send a field report, rally at a reported location, or start a roaming warband. One warband coordinates up to four normal five-player parties under a shared identity, with rally pins and group-host positions on the world map. Warbands use parties, not raids.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

- Report PvP at your current location to Warband-PvP users in your guild. Reports stay on the map for five minutes.
- Start or join a warband, set rally orders, follow the organizer's rally point, and see reported party locations.
- Rally a new warband at a field report to organize a response.
- Use optional recruitment controls inside Warband-PvP, where WoW's native Finder API permits them. Recruits still accept the game's party invitation.
- Share your location with your outdoor native group for the current session.
- Open a short-lived enemy scouting snapshot from a field report when a visible opposing Warband-PvP member returns usable information.
- Export a bounded local session journal with /wb export when reporting a problem.

THE COMMAND BOARD

The campaign frame, crimson report pins and gold rally banners bring your reports and orders together. Only one Warband-PvP dialog opens at a time. Use Back to retrace your steps or drag the header to move it. Every ordinary player feature has a UI control.

/wb - Command board
/wb report - Report PvP here
/wb show - Warbands
/wb finder - Recruitment controls
/wb export - Session journal

Optional keybindings are available through /wb and the game's Key Bindings menu. No keys are assigned automatically.

ENEMY SCOUTING: EXPERIMENTAL

Target a visible enemy before reporting. A cooperating enemy member may return their warband's name, identifier, reported membership size, party count and orders. Your same-faction guild report recipients can view that snapshot. It expires within 90 seconds of the underlying update and does not track enemy movement.

Starting or joining a warband makes your affiliation and reported size scoutable by visible enemy Warband-PvP scouts. This is disclosed beside Start / Join. The addon does not reveal non-users or hidden players. No usable reply means unknown; it does not mean the enemy has no warband.

FIRST PUBLIC ALPHA - WOW FOREVER ONLY

Built for WoW Forever 1.60.1 (Interface 16001). Other WoW clients are not supported by this build. OpenMap is optional and is not bundled.

Party invitations, native Finder behavior and cross-faction scouting delivery still need live acceptance testing. WoW may block the scouting exchange. The addon respects native restrictions and cannot select or prove a shared layer. Reported membership and location are not confirmed counts of nearby enemies or PvP participants.

The public ZIP excludes developer test commands, dummy parties and remote tester controls. It contains no external backend, advertisements or paid features. Location sharing and discovery are session-only choices. The journal stays local unless you copy and share it; it may contain character names and in-game locations.

Install the WarbandPvP folder in Interface/AddOns, restart WoW, and open /wb. If replacing a private CallToArms prototype, disable that older addon first; its commands and saved settings use different names.
