TotalWarcraft helps you quickly form parties to seek out world PvP or rally your faction to retaliate against enemy attacks in WoW Forever.

Report the attack. Rally a response. Move together.

Open /tw for your command board. Eligible outdoor PvP combat sends a field report. Rally at its location or use Create Warband to start a roaming warband. One warband coordinates up to four normal five-player parties under a shared identity, with rally pins and group-host positions on the world map. Warbands use parties, not raids.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

- Automatically report eligible outdoor PvP combat to TotalWarcraft users in your guild, with duel/NPC/instance safeguards. Manual reports use a keybind or /tw report.
- See growing conflict at a glance: compact map tooltips, native crossed-weapon icons, a pulsing red halo and a skull for heavy recent activity. Reports last five minutes; heat fades sooner.
- Start or join a warband, set rally orders, follow the organizer's rally point, and see reported party locations.
- Rally a new warband at a field report to organize a response.
- Use optional recruitment controls inside TotalWarcraft, where WoW's native Finder API permits them. Recruits still accept the game's party invitation.
- Public recruitment lists your party under the rally zone's native Competitive entry. Explicit Search lists warbands without joining them; opening the board does not start a search.
- Share your location with your outdoor native group for the current session.
- Open a short-lived enemy scouting snapshot from a field report when a visible opposing TotalWarcraft member returns usable information.
- Export a bounded local session journal with /tw export when reporting a problem.

THE COMMAND BOARD

The campaign frame, crimson report pins and gold rally banners bring your reports and orders together. Only one TotalWarcraft dialog opens at a time. Use Back to retrace your steps or drag the header to move it. Create Warband opens the rally board. Help explains features, commands and support. Manual reporting stays available by keybind or slash command; automatic reporting has its own toggle.

/tw - Command board
/tw report - Report PvP here
/tw show - Warbands
/tw finder - Recruitment controls
/tw export - Session journal
/tw auto on/off - Automatic reporting
/tw help - Guide and bug reporting

Optional keybindings are available through /tw and the game's Key Bindings menu. No keys are assigned automatically.

ENEMY SCOUTING: EXPERIMENTAL

Target a visible enemy before reporting. A cooperating enemy member may return their warband's name, identifier, reported membership size, party count and orders. Your same-faction guild report recipients can view that snapshot. It expires within 90 seconds of the underlying update and does not track enemy movement.

Starting or joining a warband makes your affiliation and reported size scoutable by visible enemy TotalWarcraft scouts. This is disclosed beside Start / Join. The addon does not reveal non-users or hidden players. No usable reply means unknown; it does not mean the enemy has no warband.

WOW FOREVER ONLY

Built for WoW Forever 1.60.1 (Interface 16001). Other WoW clients are not supported by this build. OpenMap is optional and is not bundled.

Automatic combat detection, duel safeguards and map animation need live validation. Party invitations, rally-zone Finder matching and cross-faction scouting delivery also need live acceptance testing. WoW may block the scouting exchange. The addon respects native restrictions and cannot select or prove a shared layer. Reported membership and location are not confirmed counts of nearby enemies or PvP participants.

The public ZIP excludes developer test commands, dummy parties and remote tester controls. It contains no external backend, advertisements or paid features. Location sharing and discovery are session-only choices. The journal stays local unless you copy and share it; it may contain character names and in-game locations.

Formerly Warband-PvP. /wb remains an alias for /tw. The internal addon folder and saved settings are preserved for existing users.

Install the WarbandPvP folder in Interface/AddOns, restart WoW, and open /tw. If replacing a private CallToArms prototype, disable that older addon first; its commands and saved settings use different names.

Report issues at https://github.com/KDotIV/TotalWarcraft/issues. Include your versions, steps to reproduce and /tw export before reloading.
