Reports & safeguards
Automatic reports require you and your current target to be in combat. The target must be a visible, attackable, PvP-flagged opposing player. NPCs and same-faction targets do not qualify. Duels, sanctuary/FFA rules and instances suppress automatic reports. Loading during combat waits for a fresh fight. Unavailable or restricted checks skip automatic reporting. This is a conservative observation, not proof of exchanged hits. One automatic attempt per combat; a shared two-minute cooldown follows a successful report. Guild TotalWarcraft users receive a five-minute marker. Auto reports can be toggled on /tw. Manual report: your keybind or /tw report; duel holds still apply. "Combatants" counts readable flagged players observed within 100 yards. It is partial, not a verified fight size. Unavailable counts and enemy affiliations are omitted from the tooltip.
